Maglev downloads torrents, ordinary links and the videos and files on a web page, all in one list. It is a small Android app with a pixel-art look and no ads.

<b>Torrents</b>
<ul>
<li>Open magnet links and .torrent files from a browser, a chat app, the clipboard or a share.</li>
<li>See every file before you start, pick the ones you want and mark one to download first. Maglev shows how much space will be left.</li>
<li>A queue sets how many run at once. Seeding stops at the ratio or time you choose.</li>
<li>Downloads keep going with the screen off. If Android closes the app, Maglev comes back and carries on.</li>
</ul>

<b>Any link</b>
<ul>
<li>Paste or share a web link and Maglev downloads the file, with pause, resume and retry that survive a restart.</li>
<li>Links that point at devices on your own network are stopped unless you allow them.</li>
</ul>

<b>A browser that saves what you watch</b>
<ul>
<li>Open a page inside Maglev. Ads and trackers are blocked with a built-in list of domains that you can update or replace in Settings.</li>
<li>Videos, audio, images and documents on the page show up in a tray. Tap one to download it.</li>
<li>Streaming videos (HLS and DASH) are saved as one file. Pick the quality and audio language first.</li>
<li>Maglev does not get around DRM. Protected video, live streams and players that hide their video can't be saved, and Maglev says so.</li>
</ul>

<b>Search and feeds</b>
<ul>
<li>One search box asks many public torrent sources at once and merges matching results. You can add your own Jackett or Prowlarr indexer.</li>
<li>Follow a show or add an RSS feed, and Maglev adds new episodes that match your rules.</li>
</ul>

<b>You stay in control</b>
<ul>
<li>Wi-Fi only, VPN required, pause on low battery, and download only while charging.</li>
<li>Speed limits, a schedule, and Pause all, Resume all and Shut down from the notification.</li>
<li>Files go to Download/Maglev or any folder you choose.</li>
<li>Backup and restore your settings, torrents and downloads in one file. API keys are not included.</li>
</ul>

<b>Optional assistant</b>
An assistant tab can search and explain problems in plain words. It always asks before it adds or changes anything. It is off until you add your own API key for an outside AI service (OpenRouter by default), and your messages go to that service only when you use it. Turn it off and the tab disappears.

<b>Network use</b>
Maglev talks only to the sources you search, the sites you open and the block-list addresses in Settings. It has no analytics, no ads and no account.

Maglev doesn't host or index any content. What you download, and following the law where you live, is up to you.
