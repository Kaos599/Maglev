- Maglev 1.0: the first stable release.
- Maglev now downloads almost any link (video links are experimental). Share or paste a YouTube, X or Instagram video link, pick a quality and save it. Files, magnets and torrents work as before.
- Long-press a video in the browser and choose Download.
- Swipe the address bar to switch tabs.
- A shorter page menu, Site settings and pop-up control in the browser.
- 46 short tips in Settings > Tips.
