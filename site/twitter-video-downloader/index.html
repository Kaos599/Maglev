X (Twitter) video downloader for Android

Maglev is a free, open source X (Twitter) video downloader for Android. Share or paste the link of a post with a video, and Maglev reads it with yt-dlp on your phone and shows up to 6 qualities. Pick one and it downloads into Download/Maglev. Videos that need a sign-in may not download.

Download latestHow to install

Free, GPL-3.0, no ads, no tracking, no account. Android 7 and up. Version 1.1, updated 27 November 2026.

How to download a Twitter video on Android

  1. 1

    Install Maglev

    Get the APK from GitHub releases, or add Maglev to Obtainium. Maglev needs Android 7 or later.

  2. 2

    Share or paste the link

    In X, tap Share on the post and copy its link, or share it to Maglev. In Maglev, tap + and paste the link.

  3. 3

    Pick a quality

    Maglev reads the video on your phone with yt-dlp and shows up to 6 qualities. Tap the one you want.

  4. 4

    Watch it download

    The video downloads with pause and resume, and lands in Download/Maglev.

Or use the browser

Open the post on x.com in Maglev's browser, long-press the video and choose Download.

What doesn't work

  • A video behind a sign-in may not download. Maglev says "needs sign-in" when that happens.
  • Live streams and protected video can't be saved. Maglev never gets around DRM.
  • Results vary by post and by video.

Download only what you have the right to. Maglev is a tool, and what you save is up to you.

Questions about X (Twitter) downloads

Can Maglev download X (Twitter) videos?

Public videos, often yes. Paste the post link, pick a quality and Maglev saves the video. A video behind a sign-in may not download, and Maglev says so.

Is this Twitter video downloader free?

Yes. Maglev is free, with no ads, no tracking and no account. It is open source under the GPL-3.0.

Does it work with x.com and twitter.com links?

Maglev recognises both x.com and twitter.com links, so share or paste the post link and it reads the video. If a link isn't read, open the post in Maglev's browser, long-press the video and choose Download.

Is Maglev safe, and is it open source?

The source code is public on GitHub under the GPL-3.0, and release APKs are built by GitHub Actions and signed with one key. Download it only from the official GitHub releases page or Obtainium.

Where are the videos saved?

In the Download/Maglev folder on your phone, where any file manager can open them.
