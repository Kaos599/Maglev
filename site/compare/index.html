What Maglev is

Maglev puts four things in one app: a torrent client, a download manager for ordinary links, a browser that lists the files on a page, and a way to save streaming video as one file. It is free, open source under GPL-3.0, and has no ads.

It does not download YouTube, and it does not save live streams or DRM-protected video. When a page is one it cannot save, Maglev says so.

Compared with torrent apps

LibreTorrent, BiglyBT and Flud are dedicated torrent clients. They handle magnet links, file selection and RSS feeds, and they do not include a browser or a streaming saver. LibreTorrent is open source, and BiglyBT is open source and ad-free. Flud's Play listing says it contains ads.

If you only download torrents and want a mature client with no extras, one of these is a reasonable pick. Maglev adds ordinary link downloads, a browser and a search across 40 sources, which these apps do not list.

Compared with download managers

1DM+, Advanced Download Manager (ADM) and Free Download Manager (FDM) handle links and torrents, with browsers, scheduling and speed controls. 1DM+ is listed as ad free. ADM's Play listing says it contains ads and in-app purchases. FDM is a download manager and, according to its developer, not a full web browser.

All three are closed source. Maglev is open source, and it adds an ad-free browser that saves streams and an optional assistant. ADM and 1DM+ also list real-time speed changes, and FDM lists traffic controls, so compare the speed options if that matters to you.

Compared with video downloaders

Seal and YTDLnis are open source Android front ends for yt-dlp. They can download from many sites, including YouTube, and they offer format choices and editing tools. NewPipe is an open source YouTube client that can save videos and audio.

These apps are the better choice if YouTube is what you want. Maglev does not download YouTube, and its browser and stream saver work on the other sites you open. Maglev saves HLS and DASH streams with a quality and audio language choice, which the video downloaders here do not list.

Privacy and ads

Maglev has no ads SDK and no analytics. Its browser blocks ads and trackers with a built-in list of over 42,000 domains that you can update in Settings. It contacts only the sites you open, the sources you search, the block-list addresses and OpenRouter when you use the assistant.

BiglyBT says it is ad-free, and 1DM+ says it is ad free. Flud and ADM say on their Play pages that they contain ads.

When another app is the better pick

Pick Seal, YTDLnis or NewPipe if you mainly want YouTube or music from YouTube. Pick LibreTorrent or BiglyBT if you want a torrent client with nothing else. Pick 1DM+ or ADM if you want a closed source download manager and do not mind its ads or purchases. Pick FDM if you want a desktop-style manager that also runs on Android.

Maglev is in beta, so expect rough edges. It is also not on Google Play yet, so you install it from GitHub Releases or with Obtainium.

Questions about the comparison

Is Maglev a good alternative to 1DM?

Yes, if you want links, torrents and web videos in one free app. Both Maglev and 1DM+ have no ads, scheduling and Wi-Fi-only downloads. The difference: Maglev is open source and also saves DASH and AES-128 HLS streams, while 1DM+ is closed source.

Is Maglev a good LibreTorrent alternative?

Yes, if you want torrents with RSS and file selection. Maglev also downloads ordinary links, saves streams from web pages and searches 40 built-in sources. LibreTorrent is a dedicated torrent client, and it has been around longer.

Can Maglev download YouTube videos like Seal or YTDLnis?

No. Maglev does not download YouTube. Seal and YTDLnis can, because they use yt-dlp, and NewPipe saves YouTube videos too. If YouTube is your main use, one of those apps is the better pick.

Does Maglev have ads, and do the other apps?

No, Maglev has no ads. Flud and ADM say on their Google Play pages that they contain ads. BiglyBT and 1DM+ say they are ad free, and the other apps on this page do not say either way.

Can Maglev save HLS and DASH streams?

Yes, Maglev saves on-demand HLS and DASH streams, including HLS with standard AES-128 encryption. You pick the quality and audio language, and it joins the pieces into one file. It cannot save live streams or DRM-protected video.

Which app should I choose if I only want torrents?

LibreTorrent or BiglyBT is a good pick if you only download torrents. Both are open source. Maglev works for torrents too, but it is built for links, streams and torrents together.
