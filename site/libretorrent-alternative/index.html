Maglev as an alternative to LibreTorrent

Maglev runs torrents the way LibreTorrent does, and both are open source under GPL-3.0 with no ads. Maglev also downloads video links and files, and searches torrent sources in one box. LibreTorrent's pages do not list web file downloads or a browser.

Checked 13 November 2026 against LibreTorrent's own pages and store listing. Anything those pages don't list counts as No.

Where each answer comes from

  • Maglev Open source: github.com. Almost any link, from video pages to files and torrents, in one free, open source Android app with no ads.
  • LibreTorrent Open source: github.com, source 2. A full-featured open source torrent client for Android, with RSS, scheduling and file selection.

What is the same

Both apps open magnet links and .torrent files, let you pick files inside a torrent, and follow RSS feeds with rules. Both have a Wi-Fi only setting, speed limits and a schedule. Both are free software under GPL-3.0 and have no ads.

LibreTorrent is on F-Droid, and its F-Droid listing flags no ads and no tracking.

What Maglev adds

Maglev downloads links with pause, resume and retry that survive a restart. LibreTorrent's pages describe HTTP support only for links to .torrent files.

Maglev has a built-in browser that lists the videos and files on a page, and it saves HLS and DASH streams as one file. LibreTorrent's pages do not list either.

One search box covers more than 40 public torrent sources, and Maglev can follow a show from the results. LibreTorrent's pages do not list a torrent search.

Maglev can download only over a VPN and pauses on low battery. LibreTorrent's pages do not list a VPN option. LibreTorrent's pages mention battery settings but do not say what they do.

Maglev finds subtitles for the videos in a torrent or a link download, from 21 free sources, and saves one next to the video. LibreTorrent's pages do not list subtitles.

Maglev's backup holds your settings, search sources, torrents and downloads in one file. LibreTorrent's pages list a backup of RSS channels only. Search also has a size range slider; LibreTorrent's pages do not list a torrent search.

When LibreTorrent is the better choice

Pick LibreTorrent if you only download torrents and want a client that does only that. Its F-Droid listing describes it as a full-featured torrent client with RSS, scheduling and file selection.

Pick LibreTorrent if you want to install from F-Droid. The Maglev README lists GitHub Releases and Obtainium as the ways to install it.

How to switch

Open a magnet link or a .torrent file from your browser, a chat app, the clipboard or a share, and Maglev adds it. Choose the files you want and the one to download first, then tap Start. Maglev shows how much space will be left before you start.

Downloads land in Download/Maglev. Seeding stops at the ratio or time you set. You can edit trackers, copy a magnet link or save the .torrent file from the torrent's page.

Questions about switching

Is Maglev a good LibreTorrent alternative?

Yes, if you want torrents together with links and videos in one app. If you only want torrents, LibreTorrent is a good pick, and it is on F-Droid.

Does Maglev support magnet links?

Yes. You can open a magnet link from your browser, a chat app, the clipboard or a share.

Does Maglev have RSS feeds?

Yes. Add any RSS feed, and Maglev adds new episodes that match your rules. You can also follow a show from Search.

Does Maglev seed torrents?

Yes. Seeding stops at the ratio or time you set.

Is Maglev free?

Yes. Maglev is free software under the GNU General Public License v3.0, with no ads.
