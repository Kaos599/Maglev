## What's new
- Big link downloads go faster: a file is split into 4 parts that download at the same time. Change it in Settings > Downloads > Parts per download (1, 2, 4 or 8), or for one download in its sheet. A server that can't do this gets one connection, as before.
- Free e-books from Project Gutenberg and free audiobooks from LibriVox now show up in Search. They're switched on for everyone once; turn them off in Search sources. Adding one of these opens the link download screen, since they're plain files, not torrents.
- Four more search sources, off until you turn them on in Search sources: ShowRSS (TV shows), DistroWatch (Linux), ACG.RIP (anime) and U3C3 (adult, hidden while Hide adult is on).
- Each release on GitHub has a SHA256SUMS file, so you can check a downloaded APK with `sha256sum -c`.

## What's changed
- The Pirate Bay shows only uploaders it marks as VIP or trusted. Turn off Trusted uploaders only in Search sources to see everyone's uploads.

## What's fixed
- Maglev stopped with "The connection port is already in use" when another app, such as another torrent app, was using port 6881. It now starts on a free port and says so in Settings. Your saved port isn't changed.
- Back on Search, Feeds or Settings closed the app. It now goes to Transfers, and Back on an inner screen goes to the previous one.
- A paused video showed the same triangle on two buttons. Play now has its own video icon.
- Tapping Settings very soon after opening the app could close it.
