## What's new
- A Browser screen with an address bar, back and reload. Its Files tray lists the videos, audio, images and documents on a page, so you can download one.
- Ad blocking in the browser, with a switch to allow ads on a site, and an update button in Settings → Browser.
- Save HLS and DASH streams as one file, after picking the quality and audio.
- Follow a show from Search, and its new episodes appear in Feeds.
- Open, Share and Open folder on a finished download's detail screen.

## What's changed
- Each tab icon plays a small pixel move when you select it.
- Taps answer at once, and pause, resume and remove change the row immediately.
- Transfers and Search scroll more smoothly.
- Transfers stops refreshing constantly when nothing is moving.
- The Assistant can rank results by audio language and subtitles.

## What's fixed
- A recycled row in Transfers no longer shows another torrent's time left.
