## What's new
- The browser keeps a history of the pages you open. Open it from ⋮ → History, or from Settings → Browser → History. Search it, tap a page to open it again, or remove one page or every page from a site, with Undo.
- As you type in the address bar, pages from your history show up underneath. Tap one to open it.
- Clear history for the last hour, today, the last 7 days or all time.
- "Clear history when Maglev closes" in Settings → Browser clears history when you close Maglev, and again when it next starts. It's off by default.
- Turn off Save history to stop keeping new pages, or Suggest from history to hide the address bar suggestions.
- History stays on your phone. It's not sent anywhere and it's not in backups. Pages older than 90 days are removed.

## What's changed
- The bar at the bottom of the browser is gone, so pages use the whole screen. Files and the downloading count float over the page instead.
- The number of ads and trackers blocked on a page is now in the ⋮ menu, under the ads option.
- Downloading a file or stream from the browser opens a smaller card over the page instead of a sheet across the screen.
- Notices such as "Added to Transfers." use the same dark card style as the rest of the app.
- Clear browsing data lets you choose what to clear: history, cookies and site data, or cache.

## What's fixed
- After you started one download from the browser, the "Added to Transfers." notice with View didn't appear.
