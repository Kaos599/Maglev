## What's new
- Tabs in the browser. Tap the number next to ⋮ to see your tabs, switch between them, open a new one or close them. Closing a tab, or all of them, can be undone for a few seconds. Your tabs come back after a restart.
- Long-press a link or image to open it in a new tab or a background tab, copy it, share it or download it. Long-press a magnet link to add the torrent.
- Pop-ups and redirects you didn't tap for now wait for you. A card says what the page is trying to open, with Open, Block and Always allow on this site. Settings → Browser → Pop-ups and redirects has Ask, Block without asking and Allow, and a list of sites you allowed.
- Find in page, from ⋮. It shows how many matches there are, with up and down to move between them.
- Text size for web pages, from ⋮ or Settings → Browser, from 50% to 200%.
- Maglev can update itself. It checks for a new version once a day, and Settings → About has Check for updates. Choose whether Maglev only tells you, downloads on Wi-Fi and asks before installing (the default), or installs on its own. Android asks you to confirm the first update. If you installed Maglev from F-Droid, Obtainium or another store, that store keeps updating it.
- Report a bug, in Settings → About, opens a GitHub issue with your Maglev version, Android version and phone already filled in.

## What's changed
- Result name lines moved from Search providers to Settings → Appearance, as "Search results: name lines".
- A tab you aren't looking at is paused, so its videos and sounds stop until you go back to it.
- With Save history off, open tabs aren't saved either. With "Clear history when Maglev closes" on, tabs are cleared too.
- Links that open another app (for example a Play Store or maps link) now ask first, and only when you tapped them.
- A pop-up you allow opens as a new tab. A few sign-in and payment pop-ups expect to report back to the page that opened them, and may not finish this way. If one gets stuck, use the site's own sign-in page.

## What's fixed
- A page could open many hidden windows at once and slow Maglev down. Now at most three are handled at a time and the rest are blocked.
