## What's new
- A new tab shows Recent: the last 6 pages you visited, newest first. Tap one to open it, or See all for the History screen. With Save history off, or nothing visited yet, a new tab looks as before.

## What's changed
- ⋮ in the browser works before a page is open. On a new tab it shows History and Browser settings; the page items (Find in page, Text size, Desktop site, ads and pop-ups) appear once a page loads.
- Updated libraries and build tools.

## What's fixed
- History could only be opened from ⋮, and ⋮ didn't work on a new tab, so you couldn't reach your history until a page had loaded.
