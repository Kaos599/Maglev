## What's new
- The Assistant can browse new or popular torrents by kind and time period.
- The Assistant can ask you a question before it acts.
- A "What the Assistant can do" sheet lists its abilities.
- Links in Assistant replies open Transfers with its filter, or Search with its query.
- Pick a specific free OpenRouter model for the Assistant.

## What's changed
- The Assistant chat shows each step it takes.
- The Assistant answers more briefly, and it does not say something is done until you confirm it.
- The Assistant says when OpenRouter's free daily limit is reached.

## What's fixed
- The Assistant's text box stays above the keyboard.
- A search for a title you already have points to that download instead of adding it again.
- The Assistant ignores search results whose names try to give it instructions.
