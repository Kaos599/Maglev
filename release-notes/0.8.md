## What's new
- An Assistant tab that can find and add torrents for you. It is off by default, so turn it on in Settings. Connect it with OpenRouter sign-in or your own key.
- A new Maglev train logo on the app icon and in the app, with the train cruising in the Transfers header and during onboarding.
- Search has more sources, including anime, games, books and regional sites.

## What's changed
- The provider settings in Search sit above the lists they change.
- The Settings icon is a pixel cog.
- The tab bar no longer shows a ripple when you tap a tab.

## What's fixed
- After you remove a file in the app, the Files changed notice no longer appears by mistake.
- Icon buttons no longer show a square box when pressed.
- The file selection bar on a torrent's detail screen wraps to two rows, so its labels fit.
