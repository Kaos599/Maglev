## What's new
- Stats: see what you downloaded, day by day. Open it from Settings > App > Stats. A little train runs along the week; drag it to pick a day. Time spent and average speed count while a download runs, not only when it finishes. Nothing leaves your phone.
- Stop waiting for peers after: a torrent that never starts, with no one sharing it, now gives up after 10 minutes, frees its place in the queue and shows "No one is sharing this yet" with Keep trying. Maglev tries it again every hour. Change the time, or pick Never, in Settings > Speed and sharing.
- Maglev shows up as an external download manager in browsers built on Kiwi, and downloads it gets from them keep their file names.

## What's changed
- After a search, the result count says how many sources didn't respond. Tap it to see which ones and why.

## What's fixed
- Parts per download only worked with some servers. Many sites (any that run nginx) got one connection in 0.26; they now get all the parts you set.
- A site that was down for maintenance showed "Blocked by a bot check". It now says "Down for maintenance".
- A web link typed into Add torrent asked you to tap Get info twice.
- On Android the keyboard covered the buttons in sheets such as New feed.
