## What's new
- The Assistant shows a small "Thinking…" row while it works, so you can tell it hasn't stalled.
- A dot on the Assistant tab tells you a reply finished while you were somewhere else.

## What's changed
- The Assistant keeps working when you switch tabs or leave the app.
- The Steps list opens by itself while a step runs and shows live progress, such as "Knaben answered · 12 of 28 sources".
- Each finished step keeps its detail, such as how many results came back.
- The Assistant searches for just the title. Words like "best", "movie" or "torrents" are no longer part of the search.

## What's fixed
- Stop works right away, even while the Assistant is still connecting.
- Asking for "prime time" now finds Primetime, and no longer shows other films that share one of its words.
