## What's new
- Choose your app icon from 20 designs in Settings → Appearance → App icon.
- Show, hide and reorder the tabs in Settings → Appearance → Tabs. The browser can now have its own tab.
- The Feeds tab shows how many new items are waiting.
- Tap a search result to see its details before you add it.
- "Download now on mobile data" starts one download while Wi-Fi only is on, and "Resume anyway" restarts one that your rules paused.

## What's changed
- Settings is grouped into App, Finding things, Downloads and Connection. Interface is now called Appearance, Bandwidth and Torrents & queue are one page called Speed & queue, and notifications have their own page.
- Finished torrents stop sharing on mobile data. Turn on Settings → Network → Share on mobile data to keep sharing.
- Transfers is one sorted list. Finished torrents that are still sharing show "Done · Seeding", and Clear finished is in the sort menu.
- Messages such as "Added" now show on every screen, not only on Transfers.
- Saving a file from the browser shows which folder it goes to, and tells you when you're on mobile data.
- Settings use plainer words.

## What's fixed
- Closing the quality picker no longer clears the link you were adding.
- Items marked new in Feeds stay marked until you've seen them, and Feeds refreshes when you open the tab.
