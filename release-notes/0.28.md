## What's new
- Subtitles: find and save subtitles for videos you've downloaded, from 21 free sources, with no account. For a finished link download, open its menu in Transfers; for a torrent, use its options or the ⋯ next to the video in its Files tab. Then tap Find subtitles. Results are grouped by how well they fit the file, and the subtitle is saved next to the video so players pick it up. Choose your languages and sources in Settings > Finding things > Subtitles.
- After an update, a short sheet shows the main new feature. Turn it off in Settings > Updates > Show new features after updates.
- Transfers says why downloads are held (Wi-Fi only, battery, schedule, storage full and more), with a button to change that setting.
- Search has a size range slider, so you can hide results that are too small or too big.
- Swipe a row in Transfers right to pause or resume it, or left to remove it from the list (files are kept). Turn it off in Settings > Appearance.
- Each card in Transfers shows an icon for its file type. Turn it off in Settings > Appearance > Show file type icons in Transfers.
- In the browser, swipe the address bar left or right to switch tabs, or down to see all tabs. Turn it off in Settings > Browser.
- Backups can include your stats. Tick Include stats when you export.
- Every setting can now be found with Settings search.

## What's changed
- The Downloads group in Settings is now called Transfers, like the tab, and its first page is Adding and finishing.
- The browser blocks more tricks: downloads that start without a tap go to the found-files tray instead, pages can't fill your Back history to keep you on them, known crypto-mining scripts are blocked, and pages open over HTTPS when the site supports it.

## What's fixed
- After Shut down, or after Maglev closed itself when downloads finished, adding a torrent failed with "The download engine isn't running yet". Maglev now starts the engine again by itself. If it can't, it says why and gives you a Start engine button.
- Pause all and Resume all in the notification did nothing if Android had closed Maglev in the background. They now start the engine first.
- Tapping a page in the browser could open pop-ups and pop-unders. A tap now opens a new window only for the link you tapped.
- Maglev could fail to start after Clear data when files from an earlier install were still in the download folder.
