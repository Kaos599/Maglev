## What's new
- Download any web link, not only torrents, with pause, resume and retry.
- Open a finished link download from its row in Transfers.
- Link downloads have their own filter in Transfers.
- Allow this device to download from links on your own local network servers.

## What's changed
- Links shared from other apps wait for a tap before they are added.
- The Assistant uses OpenRouter's free models by default, and you can pick another model.
- The Assistant works better with TalkBack and large text.

## What's fixed
- Copying a download never overwrites a file that is already there.
- Pause all now also pauses downloads that your rules had paused.
- A link blocked by your network settings offers an Open settings button.
