## What's new
- Add a torrent from a magnet link or a .torrent file, and pick which files to download.
- The Transfers screen shows each download's progress and speed, with pause, resume and remove.
- Search your downloads from the Transfers screen.
- Pick the folder where downloads are saved.
- Manage the queue of waiting downloads.
- See how much free space is left on the device.
- Light haptic feedback on taps.
