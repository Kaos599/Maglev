## What's new
- Share a magnet, a .torrent file or a file link to Maglev from any app. A small card shows what it is and adds it with one tap, without opening Maglev.
- Turn on "Add without asking" in Settings → Downloads to skip the card. A web page still asks, and opens Maglev's browser to find videos on it.
- Open .torrent files with Maglev from a file manager.
- Watch for a title: Maglev checks every 1, 6 or 24 hours (Wi-Fi only unless you allow mobile data) and tells you when something new turns up. Start one from Search, or ask the Assistant.
- Backup and restore in Settings: your settings, search sources, torrents and downloads in one file. API keys are never included.
- Play a video while it downloads, from its card or its screen.
- Maglev pauses downloads before the phone runs out of space (it keeps 500 MB to 1 GB free) and resumes them when space comes back. Free up space lists finished downloads you can delete.
- Assistant chats are saved and come back after a restart. A reply cut off by the restart offers Retry.

## What's changed
- When something goes wrong on a screen, Maglev shows an error screen with Reload instead of closing. About → Copy crash report copies what happened, kept only on your phone.
- "Storage full" looks the same on torrents and link downloads, with a Free up space button.
- A torrent's size counts only the files you keep, to the byte.
- Deleting a torrent and its files shows the real amount freed, including files you skipped.

## What's fixed
- A link shared to Maglev while it was closed is no longer lost.
- A finished torrent no longer shows "Files changed" after Maglev restarts.
- The Pause, Resume and Delete bar in Transfers no longer has extra space under it.
- Play on a download paused for storage says to free up space, not to resume it.
