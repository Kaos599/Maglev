## What's new
- Maglev now downloads almost any link. Share or paste a YouTube, X, Instagram or other video link, pick a quality, and Maglev saves the video. Video links are experimental: some sites may fail. If one does, tell us with Report a bug in Settings › About. Files, magnet links and torrents work as before.
- In the browser, long-press a video on YouTube, X or Instagram and choose Download.
- Maglev keeps its video-site support up to date by itself, once a day on Wi-Fi. See Settings → Updates → Video sites.
- Tips, in Settings → Tips: 46 short tips by topic. Swipe between them or pick a topic.
- Swipe the browser's address bar left or right to switch tabs.
- Tapping the address opens a full-screen editor with this page, your bookmarks and recent pages. Back leaves it.
- Site settings, from the padlock in the address bar or the page menu: ads, pop-ups and clearing this site's data in one place.
- Pop-ups: a card asks before a site opens one. Block remembers the site, and each site can be set to Ask, Allow or Block. Blocked pop-ups lists what was stopped.
- Block cookies from other sites and Try https first, in Settings → Browser → Privacy and site data.

## What's changed
- The page menu is shorter: Back, Bookmark, Reload, Share and Forward on top, then Find in page, Desktop site, Site settings, Bookmarks, History and Browser settings. Text size is in Settings → Browser → General.
- Browser settings are grouped into Browsing, Privacy and safety, Downloads, Your pages and More.
- Pull to reload follows your finger smoothly and uses Maglev's colours.
- The Transfers and Search tab icons have new animations.

## What's fixed
- Maglev now shows up in Firefox's list of download managers.
- Links shared from more browsers are found, and when one can't be read, Maglev offers to copy the details for a bug report.
- A shared link opens the page instead of being searched for.
- Find videos on this page opens the page first, and selected text on dark pages is readable.
- Downloads from servers that send the whole file in a partial reply now finish.
- A link or video download without a name no longer shows "Torrent" in its notification.
