## What's new
- Tabs in the browser. Tap the number next to ⋮ to see your tabs, switch between them, open a new one or close them, with Undo. Your tabs come back after a restart.
- Long-press a link or image to open it in a new tab or a background tab, copy it, share it or download it. Long-press a magnet link to add the torrent.
- Pop-ups and redirects you didn't tap for now wait for you, with Open, Block and Always allow on this site. Settings → Browser → Pop-ups and redirects has Ask, Block without asking and Allow.
- Find in page and text size, from ⋮ in the browser.
- The browser keeps a history you can search, from ⋮ → History. Pages you visited show up as you type in the address bar. Clear the last hour, today, the last 7 days or everything, or turn on "Clear history when Maglev closes".
- Maglev updates itself. It checks once a day, and Settings → About has Check for updates. Choose whether it only tells you, downloads on Wi-Fi and asks before installing (the default), or installs on its own. Android asks you to confirm the first update. Installs from F-Droid, Obtainium or another store keep updating through that store.
- The ad block list, the search source addresses and the default trackers refresh once a day without an app update, including ad lists you added yourself. Settings → About → Updates has Check now for each.
- Report a bug, in Settings → About, opens a GitHub issue with your Maglev version, Android version and phone filled in.

## What's changed
- The bar at the bottom of the browser is gone, so pages use the whole screen. Files and the downloading count float over the page.
- Download, stream and batch cards in the browser are smaller and match the app's dark style. The number of ads blocked on a page moved to ⋮.
- Clear browsing data lets you choose history, cookies and site data, or cache.
- Result name lines moved from Search providers to Settings → Appearance.
- A tab you aren't looking at is paused, so its video and sound stop until you go back to it.
- Links that open another app (a Play Store or maps link, for example) ask first, and only when you tapped them.
- A pop-up you allow opens as a new tab. A few sign-in and payment pop-ups expect to report back to the page that opened them and may not finish. If one gets stuck, use the site's own sign-in page.
- Once in a while, after a download finishes, Maglev asks if you'd star it on GitHub. "Not now" waits a month, and "Don't ask again" means never.

## What's fixed
- After you started one download from the browser, the "Added to Transfers." notice with View didn't appear.
- A page could open many hidden windows at once and slow Maglev down. Now at most three are handled at a time.
