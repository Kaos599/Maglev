## What's new
- Add or remove trackers on a torrent while it runs.
- Save a torrent's .torrent file, or share it.
- Remove file deletes that file's data and keeps the rest of the torrent downloading.
- Torrents that list web servers can also download from those servers.
- Search includes EZTV for TV shows, and SubsPlease and AnimeTosho for anime.

## What's changed
- Private torrents keep their trackers.
