## What's new
- Subtitles: find and save subtitles for videos you've downloaded, from 21 free sources, with no account. For a finished link download, open its menu in Transfers; for a torrent, use its options or the ⋯ next to the video in its Files tab. Then tap Find subtitles. Results are grouped by how well they fit the file, and the subtitle is saved next to the video so players pick it up. Choose your languages and sources in Settings > Finding things > Subtitles. Folders picked with the system file picker aren't supported yet.
- After an update, a short window shows the main new feature. Turn it off in Settings > Updates > Show new features after updates.
- Transfers says why downloads are held (Wi-Fi only, battery, schedule, storage full and more), with a button to change that setting.
- Search has a size range slider, so you can hide results that are too small or too big.
- Swipe a row in Transfers right to pause or resume it, or left to remove it from the list (files are kept). Turn it off in Settings > Appearance.
- Each card in Transfers shows an icon for its file type on the left. A torrent's icon comes from its biggest file. Turn it off in Settings > Appearance > Show file type icons in Transfers.
- In the browser, swipe the address bar left or right to switch tabs, or down to see all tabs. Turn it off in Settings > Browser.
- Backups can include your stats. Tick Include stats when you export.
- Every setting can now be found with Settings search.

## What's changed
- Every card in Transfers has one status line, so all cards are the same height. The left says what is happening, then the percent. The right shows the speed while data is moving, otherwise how much is downloaded or the size.
- The Downloads group in Settings is now called Transfers, like the tab, and its first page is Adding and finishing. In Settings > Subtitles, Sources is now Subtitle sources.
- The browser blocks more tricks: downloads that start without a tap go to the Files tray, pages can't fill your Back history to keep you on them, known crypto-mining scripts are blocked (even with ad blocking off), and pages open over HTTPS when the site supports it.
- Wi-Fi only says the connection is metered, not that you're on mobile data, because metered Wi-Fi and hotspots count too. A network with no internet is described as that.
- Undo after removing a row stays as long as Android's accessibility timeout asks, and never less than 5 seconds.

## What's fixed
- After Shut down, or after Maglev closed itself when downloads finished, adding a torrent failed with "The download engine isn't running yet". Maglev now starts the engine again when you add, resume or change something. If it can't, it says why and gives you a Start engine button. Background checks no longer start it, so Shut down stays shut down.
- Pause all and Resume all in the notification did nothing if Android had closed Maglev in the background. They now start the engine first, and both can show at once.
- Tapping a page in the browser could open pop-ups and pop-unders. A tap now opens a new window only for the link you tapped.
- Downloads from countdown or "preparing your file" pages now start. If a download is held, a notice offers to open Files.
- Sign-in buttons such as Continue with Google work after you tap them.
- A site that can't load over HTTPS switches to HTTP after 8 seconds, and being offline no longer marks a site as HTTP-only.
- Back no longer skips pages you opened with the Enter key.
- Only real subtitle files are saved, so a "download limit reached" page is no longer saved as a subtitle. Replacing a subtitle can't lose the old one if the save fails. A file owned by another app offers to save a second copy instead.
- Subtitle names starting with "..." work, and very long names are shortened to fit. Pick another moves on to a different subtitle, and Keep both stops after 9 copies.
- The subtitle icon on a card no longer sticks after Android closes Maglev, and a subtitle that finishes saving after you close the sheet still shows.
- The new-feature window no longer opens over a link, share or notification you opened Maglev from, and it scrolls when the text is large.
- Settings search clears itself and closes the keyboard when you come back from a result.
- Maglev could fail to start after Clear data when files from an earlier install were still in the download folder.
