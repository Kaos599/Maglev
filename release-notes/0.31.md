## What's new
- Subtitles for a whole season. See all lists one row per episode, like S02E01 · Title, with its size and what happened. Tap an episode to pick its subtitle. When episodes have a clear best match, Use the best match for 23 saves them all in one tap. A match is only saved when the title, season, episode and language agree; the rest stay for you to pick.
- When a subtitle search or a torrent search finds nothing, it shows the sources you haven't turned on, with a button to turn them on.

## What's changed
- A finished torrent whose files changed on disk says Done · Files changed instead of stopping at 99%. Resume isn't offered for it; Recheck is the way out.
- If Maglev can't resume a download, the message says why and offers Open.
- Resume all skips downloads that need a Recheck first.
- Amounts on narrow cards are shortened so the line fits.
- A download that is still starting explains what it's waiting for.

## What's fixed
- On Android 13 to 15, Back closed the app from any screen. It goes back one screen again.
- Files were changed kept coming back after Recheck. Android's media scanner and players change a file's date without changing the file; Maglev now only counts a change in size, and a Recheck that passes clears the warning for good.
- On the Stats page, picking an earlier day jumped back to today after a second while something was downloading.
- The Pixel Train city highlights the building of the day you picked, not always today's.
