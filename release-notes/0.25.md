## What's new
- Search your settings. Type "wifi", "dark" or "sd card" at the top of Settings and tap a result to open its page. Close typos work too.
- A new Updates page holds app updates and the self-updating lists, so About is just about Maglev: the star, Report a bug, the website, the licence (GPL-3.0) and what's new in your version.
- Set a speed limit for one link download from its sheet. The limit stays after a restart.
- Copy a link, open Maglev, and it offers to download it. Turn this off in Settings > Downloads if you don't want Maglev to read the clipboard.
- Paste a bare info hash (40 or 32 characters) to add a torrent.
- Choose how many parts a link download uses: 1, 2, 4 or 8 (default 4). Sites that don't allow it get one part.
- Failed downloads retry on their own after a delay you pick, up to 3 times, or keep trying. Expired links and full storage aren't retried.
- Hide from gallery keeps photo and video apps from listing Maglev's downloads.
- Four more accent colours: Violet, Blue, Amber and Mint.

## What's changed
- Sort and filter in Search has labelled sections, a toggle to hide torrents no one is sharing, and a count of what's shown. Picking a sort no longer closes it.
- Result name lines moved from Appearance to Search sources.
- Settings is regrouped into General, Finding things, Downloads, Network and power, and App. A new Downloads page holds adding downloads, parts and retry for links, and what happens when downloads finish. Speed & queue is now called Speed and sharing.
- Pink is now a magenta-pink, so it can't be mistaken for an error.
- In Light mode the Maglev logo is drawn in dark ink.
- The Settings list no longer shows a scrollbar all the time.

## What's fixed
- In Light mode the logo was light green on a light background and hard to see.
- The Settings row said "Search providers" while its page said "Search sources".
